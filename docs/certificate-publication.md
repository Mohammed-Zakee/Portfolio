# Certificate publication policy

Owner choice: watermarked previews; originals remain private.

The public catalog contains 14 distinct course/leadership certificates, with a small thumbnail and a larger watermarked derivative per credential. All certificate-image pixels include the owner name and portfolio-preview watermark. A determined person may still edit or reuse an image; this is a deterrent, not screenshot prevention.

Originals stay unchanged in the local Certificates folder, which is ignored by Git. Duplicate PNG/PDF versions, two school exam documents, and a digital sticker are not published or counted in the gallery. The temporary watermarked PDFs used to render the previews are also local-only.

Issuer verification links are provided only where the document gives a direct verification URL. The portfolio cannot restrict issuer-hosted certificate display/download behavior.

For future updates: render a watermarked derivative from the local original; update catalog.json and the embedded catalog consistently; never place an unwatermarked certificate or original PDF in public assets. Explicitly audit the staged file list before publication.
